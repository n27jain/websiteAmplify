Lying Lawyer

Born in a strict household, Vijay — a fun-loving mathematical genius — is forced into becoming a lawyer. But unlike his father's ambitions, he takes virtually no compensation from clients. All he asks for is a single favour, anything he wants, redeemable at any time. Since favours carry no legal weight, every client agrees.

What does he want? What are his true intentions? In this twisted game of justice, where good and bad are never black and white, who truly wins — those who achieve justice, or those who follow the path of justice? We all have secrets that could condemn us in the right society. But what if one builds their own?
